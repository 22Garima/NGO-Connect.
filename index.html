Why NGOConnect

Making community support easier to discover

Needs and resources can be difficult to bring together. NGOConnect provides a shared online space to communicate requirements and explore potential connections.

Visibility

Make needs easier to find

NGOs and individuals can describe what support they are looking for, from essential supplies to volunteer help.

Resource sharing

Help resources find direction

Providers can share information about available items and resources for others to discover.

Connection

Encourage meaningful connections

A clearer view of needs and offers can help people decide how they may participate.

Who We Are

A shared space for people who need support and people ready to offer it.

NGOConnect is a community-focused platform concept designed to help NGOs, resource providers and individuals share information about needs and available resources.

Our goal is to make resource discovery more accessible through a simple online experience that keeps community support at the centre.

Community Stories

Every act of care has a story.

Examples of how a resource-sharing platform may help people discover opportunities to support one another.

Illustrative example

Sharing essential supplies

An organisation lists a need for food supplies while a provider shares information about available items. Both can explore whether the offer meets the requirement.

Illustrative example

Giving useful items a purpose

A contributor lists clothing in good condition so others can explore whether those items fit a current requirement.

Illustrative example

Making room for volunteers

An organisation describes a volunteer requirement, helping interested people learn about the opportunity and explore how they could contribute.

These are illustrative scenarios, not verified testimonials or confirmed outcomes.
